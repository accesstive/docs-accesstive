Some items in the dashboard are marked **SOON**. You can see them, but you cannot open or use them yet.

## In the menu

| Item | Menu | What it is about |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **File Remediation** | **FIX** | Making files on your website, such as PDF documents, accessible. |
| **VPAT** | **PROVE** | VPAT/ACR documents. A VPAT is a standard form for an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR), which companies often ask for when they buy software. |
| **Litigation Support** | **PROVE** | Help if you receive a legal complaint or demand letter about the accessibility of your website. |

## In the widget settings

In **FIND** → **Widget**, these feature cards are shown but cannot be turned on yet:

- **Image Descriptions**: descriptive text that gives context for images
- **Language Option**: lets visitors switch between available languages
- **Instant Mode**: quicker responses in the widget
