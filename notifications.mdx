description: "What Accesstive tells you in the dashboard and by email, and when."
---

Accesstive keeps you up to date in two ways: notifications in the dashboard and emails.

## Dashboard notifications

Click the notifications icon in the top bar to open your notifications. You get one when, for example:

- A page check or Monitor check is complete
- A Lighthouse scan has started or is completed
- Your trial or subscription has started
- Your subscription will expire soon (30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 day before) or has expired

Short messages also appear at the top of the screen right after an action, for example "Editor updated successfully." or "This page already exists."

## Emails

| Email | When you get it |
| --- | --- |
| Login details | A teammate gets their login details when you add them in [Team](/team). |
| Report ready | Your pages were checked and the report is ready to view in the dashboard. |
| Password reset | You clicked **Forgot your password?** on the login page. |

You may also get emails about your trial, your plan, and invoices.

If your website grows past the widget page limit of your plan, the Accesstive team contacts you about upgrading.

<Tip>
  Add info@accesstive.com to your address book so Accesstive emails do not land in spam.
</Tip>
