## Product

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Accesstive?">
    Accesstive is a tool that helps you find accessibility problems on your website, fix them, watch the site over time, and keep a record of that work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is accessibility compliance management?">
    It is the ongoing process of finding issues, managing remediation, monitoring websites, tracking progress, and keeping documentation to support accessibility standards and regulatory requirements—not a one-time scan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I start with just the Accessibility Widget?">
    Yes. The Accessibility Widget is included in every paid plan from Solo up. You can also start with a free accessibility scan. As needs grow, add monitoring, remediation, reporting, and documentation tools.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is Accesstive different from a one-time accessibility audit?">
    A one-time audit is a snapshot. Accesstive supports ongoing management through continuous monitoring, remediation tracking, reporting, and documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Accesstive guarantee compliance?">
    No tool can guarantee legal compliance. Accesstive helps you detect issues, manage fixes, and document work. Records show work your team has done—not a compliance status. Complex screen-reader and keyboard flows often need human review.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Live Audit and monitoring

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Live Audit work?">
    Live Audit runs on your live page. Issues appear on screen where they exist, with the failing element, AI fix guidance, and disability impact. You can start from the free [Live Audit<span style={{position: "absolute", width: "1px", height: "1px", overflow: "hidden", clipPath: "inset(50%)", whiteSpace: "nowrap"}}> (opens in a new tab)</span>](https://accesstive.com/live-audit/) page or from **FIND** → **Live Audit** in the dashboard. See [Live Audit](/live-audit).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the Monitor work?">
    Accesstive checks your monitored pages again every day, so you notice new problems after website changes. Add pages under **FIND** → **Monitor**. See [Scanner and Monitor](/monitor).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why isn't finding accessibility issues enough?">
    Finding issues is only the first step. You also need to prioritize remediation, track progress, monitor changes, and maintain documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will this break my website design?">
    Accessibility improvements work alongside your existing design. Better contrast, cleaner markup, and keyboard navigation do not require changing how your site looks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Widget and Fix Hub

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the Accessibility Widget make my site compliant by itself?">
    No. The widget helps visitors change how your site looks and reads. It does not fix the code of your website. Use Scanner, Monitor, Fix Hub, and a human review as well. See [Accessibility Widget](/widget).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will Fix Hub fix my website automatically?">
    No. Fix Hub can propose AI-generated code fixes. Your developer reviews and applies each change. Nothing is applied to your website code automatically. See [Fix Hub](/fix-hub).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Fix Hub close fixed issues by itself?">
    No. After a fix, check the page again. If the issue is gone, set it to **Complete** yourself. See [Verify and close](/fix-hub#verify-and-close).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does Fix Hub get its issue list?">
    Every issue found by the Scanner and Monitor goes into Fix Hub automatically, sorted by severity and linked to its WCAG rule. You do not need to import anything.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing and experts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does pricing work?">
    Plans are based on pages, not traffic. See the [Accesstive pricing page<span style={{position: "absolute", width: "1px", height: "1px", overflow: "hidden", clipPath: "inset(50%)", whiteSpace: "nowrap"}}> (opens in a new tab)</span>](https://accesstive.com/pricing/) for current prices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. New sign-ups get a 14-day Starter trial, no card needed. Plans bought in the dashboard are charged right away. See [Free trial](/billing#free-trial).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Accesstive fully automated?">
    No. Accesstive combines automation, continuous monitoring, and human accessibility support. Automated tools detect and track issues. Human experts can review complex patterns, such as screen reader and keyboard flows, on demand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can Accesstive human experts do?">
    Experts can audit your site by hand, help fix issues, produce VPAT and ACR documentation (standard accessibility reports used when companies buy software), and support you after a legal complaint. Book a specialist in **FIX** → **Human Expert Audit**, or contact the team for a quote. See [Human Expert Audit](/human-expert-audit).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We already have a developer. Why do we need Accesstive?">
    Your developer builds features. Accessibility needs specialist knowledge of WCAG, assistive technology, and legal standards—a different skill set. Accesstive gives structure, detection, and expert support around that work.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and privacy

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the Accessibility Widget use cookies?">
    No. The widget does not set cookies. Visitor choices are saved only in the visitor's own browser (local storage). See [Accessibility Widget](/widget#privacy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Accesstive on more than one website?">
    Yes. Each plan covers 1 website. Add another website with **Add New Website**. Agencies can ask for a custom offer. See [Plans and billing](/billing#add-another-website).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add my team?">
    Open **Team** and click **Add New User**. Your teammate gets login details by email. See [Team](/team).
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The widget does not show on my website">
    - Check that the code from **FIND** → **Widget** → **Installation Guide** is on every page, just before the closing `</body>` tag.
    - Use the code from your own dashboard. The `data-token` connects the widget to your website.
    - Clear your website cache (and ask your host to clear their extra cache, called a CDN, if you use one), then reload the page.
    - If it still does not show, email support, or ask your developer to look for errors in the browser.
    - Check that your plan is active in **Account / Billing**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My site is behind a password, and checks do not work">
    Accesstive cannot check pages that ask for a username and password before the page loads. Live Audit shows "The URL is secured with HTTP Basic Authentication, which prevents this feature from working." Remove the password prompt, or check a public copy of the site.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My plan has expired">
    The dashboard shows "Your subscription has ended." Checks, Live Audit, and adding teammates stop. Click **Renew** in the top bar or in **Account / Billing**. See [Renew an expired plan](/billing#renew-an-expired-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A check did not return a report">
    You see "The accessibility check did not return a report." Wait a moment and try again. Make sure the page is public and loads in a normal browser. If it keeps failing, contact support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot add a page to the Monitor">
    The page address must start with your website address. On Solo only your home page is monitored. If you see "Upgrade required", you have used all monitored pages in your plan. See [Scanner and Monitor](/monitor#common-messages).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
