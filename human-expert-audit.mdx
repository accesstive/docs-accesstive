Some accessibility problems only show up when a real person uses your website, for example with a screen reader or only a keyboard. With **Human Expert Audit**, a trained specialist checks your website and confirms these issues.

## What a person finds that a scanner cannot

| Issue type | Scanner | Human expert |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing alt text | Detected | Detected and reviewed |
| Color contrast | Detected | Detected, with context |
| Keyboard trap logic | Not detected | Fully reviewed |
| Screen reader flow | Not detected | Fully reviewed |
| Form use with a screen reader | Not detected | Fully reviewed |
| Too much mental effort (cognitive load) | Not detected | Fully reviewed |

The expert works alongside your automatic checks. Your Monitor keeps running while the specialist reviews new and changed pages on a schedule you agree on.

## Book a specialist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the module">
    Go to **FIX** → **Human Expert Audit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a time">
    Click **Book Your Specialist** and choose a time in the booking calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to the expert">
    In the call, you agree on which pages to review and how often.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Book a review before you get a complaint. Accesstive also offers separate support if you already received a legal demand letter. See [Expert Services](https://accesstive.com/expert-services/).
</Tip>
